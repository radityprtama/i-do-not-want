# Privacy Policy Review

Applicable when personal information is collected, received, inferred, stored
or shared.

Build:

```text
data category
-> source
-> purpose
-> destination/processor
-> retention (only if known)
-> user-facing disclosure
```

## Checks
- `LEGAL-PRIV-001` HIGH: material personal-data collection/sharing lacks a
  relevant user-facing privacy notice where one is expected.
- `LEGAL-PRIV-002` HIGH/MEDIUM: notice materially omits or contradicts observed
  processing.
- `LEGAL-PRIV-003` MEDIUM: rights/contact instructions represented by the
  product are missing/broken.
- `LEGAL-PRIV-004` MEDIUM: retention claims are invented or contradict actual
  implementation.

Do not manufacture retention periods or legal bases. Identify unresolved
business/legal questions explicitly.
