# Copyright, Licenses & Assets

Inventory:
- photos/video/audio;
- illustrations;
- icons/SVGs;
- fonts;
- copied prose;
- datasets;
- templates;
- third-party code with distribution obligations;
- trademarks/logos.

Classify provenance:

`OWNED | LICENSED | OPEN_LICENSE | PUBLIC_DOMAIN | PERMISSION_RECORDED | UNKNOWN`

## Checks
- `LEGAL-IP-001` MEDIUM/WARN: third-party asset provenance/license is unknown.
- `LEGAL-IP-002` HIGH/MEDIUM: known license appears incompatible with intended
  distribution/use.
- `LEGAL-IP-003` MEDIUM: required attribution/license notice is missing.
- `LEGAL-IP-004` MEDIUM: third-party mark/logo could misleadingly imply
  affiliation.

A filename containing `free`, `unsplash` or `stock` is not license evidence.
