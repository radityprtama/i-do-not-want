# Terms & User-Generated Content

Applicable when users create accounts, publish/upload content, collaborate,
buy a service, or otherwise need product rules.

For UGC inspect:
- ownership/license wording;
- visibility defaults;
- prohibited-content/process;
- abuse/reporting path;
- moderation/takedown workflow where relevant;
- account suspension/deletion behavior.

## Checks
- `LEGAL-TERMS-001` WARN/MEDIUM: terms are absent where the service relationship
  creates meaningful unresolved expectations/risk.
- `LEGAL-TERMS-002` HIGH/MEDIUM: terms promise behavior the product does not
  provide.
- `LEGAL-UGC-001` HIGH/MEDIUM: foreseeable UGC abuse has no operational
  reporting/moderation path.
- `LEGAL-UGC-002` MEDIUM: content ownership/license representations conflict.

Governing law, venue, arbitration and liability clauses are business/legal
decisions. Never invent them.
