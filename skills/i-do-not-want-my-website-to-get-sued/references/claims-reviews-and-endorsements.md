# Claims, Reviews & Endorsements

AI-generated landing pages love fictional social proof. Do not treat it as
decoration.

Search user-facing content for:
- "trusted by X";
- customer/user counts;
- star ratings;
- testimonials;
- customer/partner/press logos;
- "#1", "best", "fastest", "most secure";
- uptime/performance/revenue/savings claims;
- awards/certifications;
- guarantees;
- before/after results.

For objective claims, locate substantiation supplied by product evidence.

## Checks
- `LEGAL-CLAIM-001` HIGH/MEDIUM: material factual/quantitative claim has no
  substantiation in available evidence.
- `LEGAL-REVIEW-001` HIGH: testimonial/review provenance cannot be established
  and appears generated/placeholder.
- `LEGAL-LOGO-001` HIGH/MEDIUM: logo presentation implies a customer/partner/
  press relationship that cannot be substantiated.
- `LEGAL-CERT-001` HIGH: compliance/certification badge appears without evidence.
- `LEGAL-DISCLOSE-001` MEDIUM: paid/incentivized endorsement lacks an applicable
  disclosure.

Do not accuse a real person/company of fraud. Say substantiation was not found.
